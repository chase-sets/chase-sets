# Marketplace Production Promotion

Production remains a landing and admin-support deployment until marketplace promotion is explicitly approved. Do not set `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PUBLIC_ENABLED=true` until every gate below has an accountable owner and passing evidence, and do not mark `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PROMOTION_APPROVED=true` until the final launch review record exists. Keep the redacted [Marketplace Launch Evidence](./marketplace-launch-evidence.md) packet passing before copying its values into the production GitHub Environment.

## Required Gates

- Public Presence: prelaunch copy is replaced or reviewed for live marketplace availability, and terms, privacy, refunds and returns, order protection, and sales fee pages no longer describe live transactions as future-only.
- Tax: production Tax readiness evidence is approved, `PRODUCTION_TAX_READINESS_APPROVED=true`, `PRODUCTION_TAX_READINESS_REFERENCE` points to the approval record, and Tax nexus tracking confirms either no jurisdiction currently requires collection or `TAX_PROVIDER_BACKED_QUOTES_REQUIRED=true` is paired with a provider-backed Tax Quote resolver before live order creation.
- Payments: counsel/provider review approves buyer-facing Marketplace Checkout Fee copy, state-specific disclosures, refund language, and Stripe live-mode configuration; `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_CHECKOUT_FEE_APPROVED=true` and `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_CHECKOUT_FEE_REFERENCE` point to the Payments approval record.
- Payments and Settlement: Stripe live checkout, refunds, disputes, Connect onboarding, manual payout schedule, payout readiness, payout holds, platform-balance funding, webhooks, Radar/risk posture, payout failure reversal, and reconciliation are verified; `PRODUCTION_STRIPE_MONEY_OPERATIONS_APPROVED=true` and `PRODUCTION_STRIPE_MONEY_OPERATIONS_REFERENCE` point to the live money operations record.
- Fulfillment: EasyPost production mode, label purchase, label refund/void, tracking, exception workflows, and Letter Mailpiece handling are verified; `PRODUCTION_FULFILLMENT_POSTAGE_APPROVED=true` and `PRODUCTION_FULFILLMENT_POSTAGE_REFERENCE` point to the Fulfillment postage readiness record.
- Support: account support can open structured buyer and seller order issues, operations can review overdue or urgent requests at `/operations/support-requests`, refund-producing resolutions are rehearsed against staging, `PRODUCTION_SUPPORT_OPERATIONS_APPROVED=true`, and `PRODUCTION_SUPPORT_OPERATIONS_REFERENCE` points to the Support readiness record.
- Notifications: `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_PROVIDER=amazon-ses` is enabled with complete production SES values; DNS, controlled sends, outbox delivery, bounce/complaint handling, and magic link, order, payment, fulfillment, refund, support, and payout notices are verified; `PRODUCTION_TRANSACTIONAL_EMAIL_APPROVED=true` and `PRODUCTION_TRANSACTIONAL_EMAIL_REFERENCE` point to the Notifications transactional email readiness record.
- Catalog and Ordering: active launch supply has resolved product measurement coverage for every checkout-eligible listing, the measurement sweep reports zero missing measures, and `PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_SUPPLY_MEASUREMENTS_APPROVED=true` plus `PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_SUPPLY_MEASUREMENTS_REFERENCE` point to the Catalog-owned sweep record.
- UCP/AP2: agent-commerce and headless-checkout claims stay out of public launch marketing unless a separate UCP/AP2 certification record proves production verifier, merchant signing key, provider-backed Stripe Shared Payment Token, OAuth, signed-write, trusted-handoff, incident-response, support, and finance readiness.
- Staging: deployed staging critical flows and `pnpm run stripe:money-smoke -- --edge-check --seller-flow` pass for the same release commit.
- Production: the production workflow validates approved launch evidence, approved Marketplace Checkout Fee evidence, approved Stripe money operations evidence, approved Support operations evidence, approved Fulfillment postage evidence, approved transactional email evidence, approved launch supply measurement evidence, approved Tax readiness evidence, live Stripe keys, EasyPost production mode, production Connect URLs, SES configuration, marketplace domain readiness, and marketplace smoke before tagging the release.

## Promotion Switch

The production deploy uses `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PUBLIC_ENABLED` from the production GitHub Environment. Keep it unset or `false` for the current launch posture.

When set to `true`, Terraform deploys the production marketplace surface, full platform API, platform worker, and commerce bounded-context databases. The same switch also requires approved marketplace promotion evidence, approved Marketplace Checkout Fee evidence, approved Stripe money operations evidence, approved Support operations evidence, approved Fulfillment postage evidence, approved transactional email evidence, approved launch supply measurement evidence, live payment/shipping/email configuration, and approved Tax readiness evidence before Terraform can plan.

Marketplace promotion evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PROMOTION_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PROMOTION_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. The reference must point to the final launch review record that confirms each required gate above has an accountable owner and passing evidence. `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PUBLIC_ENABLED=true` is only the deployment shape switch; it is not the launch approval by itself.

Marketplace Checkout Fee evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_CHECKOUT_FEE_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_CHECKOUT_FEE_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Payments has a counsel/provider-reviewed record covering buyer-facing fee copy, fee labels, refund handling language, state-specific disclosures, and Stripe live-mode configuration.

Stripe money operations evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_STRIPE_MONEY_OPERATIONS_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_STRIPE_MONEY_OPERATIONS_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Payments, Settlement, Finance, Operations, and Support have a live-mode Stripe record covering checkout, capture, refunds, disputes, Connect onboarding, payout readiness, platform-balance funding, transfer and payout execution, `payout.paid`, `payout.failed`, duplicate webhook replay, provider reconciliation, Radar/risk rules, and rollback actions.

Support operations evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_SUPPORT_OPERATIONS_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_SUPPORT_OPERATIONS_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Support has a staging rehearsal record covering account issue opening, admin queue review, overdue escalation, evidence/response/resolution/close/cancel endpoints, refund-producing resolution visibility, settlement holds, and support notification delivery.

Fulfillment postage evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_FULFILLMENT_POSTAGE_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_FULFILLMENT_POSTAGE_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Fulfillment has a production EasyPost rehearsal record covering live-mode provider configuration, parcel label purchase, label void/refund, tracking update handling, delivery exception handling, and Letter Mailpiece preparation without a parcel-label purchase.

Transactional email evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_TRANSACTIONAL_EMAIL_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_TRANSACTIONAL_EMAIL_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Notifications has a production Amazon SES rehearsal record covering DNS, controlled sends, outbox delivery, bounce/complaint handling, delivery monitoring, and critical transactional templates for magic link, account security, order, payment, fulfillment, refund, support, and payout notices.

Launch supply measurement evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_SUPPLY_MEASUREMENTS_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_LAUNCH_SUPPLY_MEASUREMENTS_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until Catalog has a production data-quality sweep proving active checkout-eligible launch listings have `activeLaunchListingCount > 0`, `activeLaunchListingsMissingResolvedProductMeasures=0`, and `resolvedProductMeasureCoveragePercent=100`. Ordering keeps the runtime checkout blocker for missing measures; this gate prevents opening public checkout with avoidable listing failures.

Tax readiness evidence is carried by `PRODUCTION_TAX_READINESS_APPROVED` and `PRODUCTION_TAX_READINESS_REFERENCE` in the production GitHub Environment. Keep approval unset until the Tax-owned launch posture has counsel/accounting review, state-by-state nexus tracking, and a documented collection-provider decision. `TAX_PROVIDER_BACKED_QUOTES_REQUIRED=false` is allowed only while the Tax readiness record confirms no tracked jurisdiction requires collection. When a jurisdiction becomes registered for collection or otherwise collection-required, set `TAX_PROVIDER_BACKED_QUOTES_REQUIRED=true`; Platform API then installs a production Tax blocker resolver until a provider-backed `TaxQuoteResolver` is composed.

## Rollback Posture

If promotion is enabled and production marketplace smoke fails, set `PRODUCTION_MARKETPLACE_PUBLIC_ENABLED=false` and redeploy the last known safe commit. That returns production to landing/admin-support while preserving staging as the full commerce verification environment.
