# 30-Day Campaign Content Calendar

The campaign runs from 2026-07-20 through 2026-08-18. Campaign day 1 is the Wave 0 milestone date; it is distinct from Invite Wave 1 on 2026-07-31. Invite Wave 2 targets 2026-08-07 and Invite Wave 3 targets 2026-08-14, but both remain ops-gated rather than public promises. Public copy continues to say “late July 2026” for beta waves; September 1, 2026 is the only hard public launch date.

Every slot must pass [Offer Economics Claims Substantiation](./offer-economics-claims-substantiation.md) immediately before publishing. Status `ready` means publishable text exists after its named gate; it does not mean operator creative, live evidence, or scheduling is complete.

## Reference key

- `C1` — Claim 1 softened wording only; no percentages, cap, or competitor comparison.
- `C2` — Claim 2 exact Order Protection wording.
- `C3` — Claim 3 softened graded-card wording; no self-serve UI or per-grade pricing implication.
- `C4` — Claim 4 open-offer wording/mechanics.
- `C5` — Claim 5 generator output; production only. Fixture/staging means hold.
- `NC` — no product/economics claim: founder process, question, recap, or event logistics.

## Week-one copy deck

These are publishable text structures once the named gate is satisfied. TODD owns imagery, video, timing, replies, community-rule review, and any founder-specific facts inserted in brackets.

**Day 1 — X kickoff (NC)**

> I'm building Chase Sets for people who buy and sell cards and want a clearer marketplace workflow. For the next 30 days I'll share what we're building, what the data actually says, and what changes after collector feedback. First question: what slows you down most today—finding a card, pricing it, or getting a deal done?

**Day 2 — X fee principle (C1)**

> No listing fees, no fixed per-item charge on our published rate — you only pay when you sell. That's the wording we can substantiate today, and we'll keep publishing the evidence when the rate changes.

**Day 3 — X market-data boundary (C5)**

If a production product clears the generator's gates, use its unedited X thread instead. Otherwise publish this nonnumeric fallback:

Until production has that evidence, we'll explain the method instead of presenting fixture data as marketplace activity. Product-level only—not a game-wide index or per-grade price guide.
