This is a greenfield project. Breaking changes are okay. System is scalable, maintainable, event-sourced, event-driven, follows domain driven design principles, prioritizes clarity and simplicity, uses vertical slicings, favors single responsibility, composition over inheritance, functional patterns and is self-explanatory. The design system is the canonical source of truth for all UI components and patterns with no custom overrides. The marketplace is designed to scale and accommodate a wide range of buyers and sellers, with a focus on competitive pricing, efficient workflows, and better margins for low-value cards. Terms use natural language and match both internal and external uses.
