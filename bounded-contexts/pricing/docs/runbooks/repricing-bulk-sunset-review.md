# Bulk Reprice Sunset Review

Review the Pricing source-share panel weekly after the UTC week is complete.
Only `complete` weeks are eligible for the configured consecutive-week window.
An `incomplete` week, an `unattributed` event, a missing port, or an unknown
policy is an explicit hold, never a zero or a manual event.

The reviewer records the policy revision, week range, bulk/policy/manual counts
and shares, top sellers above the reliance threshold, and the evaluator result.
Do not delete or disable bulk reprice from this review. Removal remains a
pre-authored future gate after measured adoption and seller migration evidence.
