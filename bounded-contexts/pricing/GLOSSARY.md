# Pricing Domain Glossary

This glossary defines the canonical terminology for the Pricing bounded context.

## Price Signal

A **Price Signal** is an observed market input used to estimate fair value.

Examples:

- Active listings
- Accepted offers
- Completed orders

## Market Price Estimate

A **Market Price Estimate** is the modeled fair-value output for a catalog item over a defined time window.

## Liquidity Estimate

A **Liquidity Estimate** is the modeled expectation of how quickly or reliably an item can transact.

## Repricing Policy

A **Repricing Policy** is the rule set that turns pricing inputs into seller-facing recommendations.

## Price Recommendation

A **Price Recommendation** is the suggested listing or offer strategy generated by Pricing.

## Historical Pricing Snapshot

A **Historical Pricing Snapshot** is a stored pricing output used for trend analysis and forecasting.
