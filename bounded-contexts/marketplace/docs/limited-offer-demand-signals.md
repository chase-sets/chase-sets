# Limited Offer Demand Signals

Marketplace Offers remain the source of truth for submitted product-wide demand. Full Offer detail stays visible through Marketplace-owned workflows such as Submitted Offers and Offer Matches.

Discovery Product Alerts may notify a subscribed account that matching offer demand exists for a Product when the offer satisfies the alert's market side and minimum price threshold.

The Product Alert signal is intentionally limited. It may include product identity, product summary, and matching price. It must not include buyer identity, shipping destination, buyer contact details, or the full Offer record unless the account also has Marketplace visibility through an Offer Match or another explicit Marketplace workflow.
