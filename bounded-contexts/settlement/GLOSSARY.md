# Settlement Domain Glossary

This glossary defines the canonical terminology for the Settlement bounded context.

## Wallet

A **Wallet** is the balance container for an organization within the marketplace ledger.

## Ledger Entry

A **Ledger Entry** is the atomic accounting record that explains a balance change.

## Balance

A **Balance** is the current computed financial position of an organization derived from ledger entries.

## Payout

A **Payout** is the transfer of eligible marketplace funds to an organization.

## Payout Batch

A **Payout Batch** is a grouped payout execution run.

## Statement

A **Statement** is the financial summary published for an organization over a period of time.
